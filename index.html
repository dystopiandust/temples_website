1. Rumtek Monastery

Location

Gangtok, Sikkim, India

Significance

Rumtek Monastery, also known as the Dharma Chakra Centre, is the largest monastery in Sikkim and serves as the primary seat-in-exile of the Gyalwang Karmapa of the Karma Kagyu school of Tibetan Buddhism.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: March to June
Nearest Airport: Pakyong Airport, Sikkim
Nearest Railway Station: New JalpaiGuri
Opening Hours: 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM

2. Golden Temple

Location

Amritsar, Punjab, India

Significance

The Golden Temple in Amritsar, Punjab, is the holiest shrine in Sikhism, renowned worldwide for its spiritual radiance, open-door policy, and architectural beauty

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to March
Nearest Airport: Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport
Nearest Railway Station: Amritsar Railway Station
Opening Hours: 24 Hours

3. Ranganathswamy Temple

Location

Srirangam, Tamil Nadu, India

Significance

The Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple in Srirangam, Tamil Nadu, is the world's largest functioning Hindu temple complex, spanning 156 acres on an island bounded by the Kaveri and Kollidam rivers.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: Month
Nearest Airport: Trichy Airport
Nearest Railway Station: Srirangam Railway Station
Opening Hours: 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM

4. Padmanabhaswamy Temple

Location

City, State, India

Significance

The Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple is a famous Hindu shrine located in East Fort, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, dedicated to Lord Vishnu in an 18-foot-long reclining posture on the serpent Anantha.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to February
Nearest Airport: Trivandrum International Airport
Nearest Railway Station: Thiruvananthapuram Central Railway Station
Opening Hours: Morning sessions run from 3:15 AM to 12:00 PM; evening sessions run from 5:00 PM to 7:20 PM (subject to change on festival days).

5. St. Paul's Cathedral

Location

Kolkata, West Bengal, India

Significance

in Kolkata is an iconic 19th-century Indo-Gothic landmark and the first Episcopal church built in the overseas territory of the British Empire. Consecrated in 1847, it was the first cathedral constructed in the overseas territory of the British Empire and the first Anglican cathedral in Asia.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to February
Nearest Airport: Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport
Nearest Railway Station: Howrah Junction
Opening Hours: Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM–12:00 PM and 3:00 PM–6:00 PM; Sunday from 7:30 AM–6:00 PM

6. Mount Mary Church

Location

Mumbai, Maharashtra, India

Significance

Known as the "Shrine of Wishes," housing a revered statue of Mother Mary that attracts millions of devotees across various religions.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: September
Nearest Airport: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport
Nearest Railway Station: Bandra Station
Opening Hours: 6:00 AM to 9:00/10:00 PM

7. Nandroling Monastery

Location

Bylakuppe, Karnataka, India

Significance

The Namdroling Monastery in Bylakuppe, Karnataka, is the largest teaching center of the Nyingma lineage of Tibetan Buddhism in the world.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to March
Nearest Airport: Mysore Airport (MYQ)
Nearest Railway Station: Mysuru Junction (MYS)
Opening Hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

8. Khajuraho Temple

Location

City, State, India

Significance

Famous worldwide for their stunning stone carvings, deities, celestial maidens (Apsaras), and famous expressive art depicting various facets of life, including human emotions and intimate sculptures.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to March
Nearest Airport: Khajuraho Airport (HJR)
Nearest Railway Station: Khajuraho Railway Station (KURJ)
Opening Hours: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily.

9. Dilwara Temples

Location

Mount Abu, Rajasthan, India

Significance

Built by ministers and rulers like Vimal Shah and Vastupal-Tejpal under the Chalukya dynasty, the temples look plain on the outside but feature extraordinarily detailed ceiling carvings, ornamental pillars, and doorways inside.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to March
Nearest Airport: Maharana Pratap Airport in Udaipur
Nearest Railway Station: Abu Road Railway Station
Opening Hours: 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM

10. Ranakpur Temple

Location

Ranakpur, Rajasthan, India

Significance

The Ranakpur Jain Temple in Rajasthan is a world-famous 15th-century marble masterpiece dedicated to Tirthankara Adinatha.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to March
Nearest Airport: Maharana Pratap Airport, Udaipur
Nearest Railway Station: Falna Railway Station (FA)
Opening Hours: 12:00 PM (noon) to 5:00 PM
